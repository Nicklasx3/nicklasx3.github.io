Otter Flip

Dive, flip and rescue baby otters.

Otter Flip is a one-touch arcade game for iPhone. Dive deep to build speed, burst out of the water, flip in the air, pick up lost baby otters and carry them to the next boathouse before the orca catches you.

Common questions

I bought Remove ads or the Starter pack and reinstalled the game.
Open Settings (the gear on the main menu) and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

My shells are gone after reinstalling.
Game progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Shell packs are used when bought and can't be restored.

A purchase went wrong.
Payments are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I want to see the tutorial again.
Open Settings and tap Replay tutorial. It plays on your next dive.

There is no sound or vibration.
Turn off silent mode on your iPhone and check that Sound and Vibration are on in the game's Settings.
