Privacy Policy

Otter Flip for iPhone · Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Otter Flip game ("the app") uses and why. The app is made by an independent developer ("we", "us").

Short version

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your iPhone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Shells, upgrades, missions, settings and records are saved locally on your iPhone. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show video and full-screen ads. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device information such as model, operating system, language and IP address (which gives an approximate location);
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the consent form, and on iPhone under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on our own servers. For data collected by Google or Apple, you can use the controls and rights described in their privacy policies. Residents of the EEA may also contact their local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
